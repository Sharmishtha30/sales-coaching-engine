# Fictional scripts for recording

These are authored development examples. Names/products are fictional. No recordings or real customer data are included. Timings in program fixtures are illustrative; annotate actual timings after recording.

## Call 1 — discovery

Maya: Hello, um, I am Maya from FlowDesk. What is slowing your team down today?

Buyer: We copy customer requests between email and spreadsheets. We lose about two hours every day.

Maya: Uh, our product has dashboards and automation. Um, it costs eighty dollars per user.

Buyer: Does it connect to our existing helpdesk? We cannot replace that system.

Maya: I need to check that integration. Could you tell me which helpdesk you use?

Buyer: We use HelpBox. Can you send me the details?

Maya: Yes, I will check the integration and email you tomorrow.

Human review prompts: Did Maya understand the workflow before pitching? Was the price relevant yet? Was the integration uncertainty handled honestly? Did the next step have an owner and time? Are fillers actually disruptive when you hear them?

## Call 2 — follow-up on the same deal

Maya: Last time you mentioned two hours of copying requests each day. Is that still the main issue?

Buyer: Yes, and our manager needs to see requests that have not been assigned.

Maya: The assignment dashboard can show that. HelpBox integration is not available yet, so I cannot promise to remove the copying work.

Buyer: Then we should wait. The integration matters more than another dashboard.

Maya: Understood. May I contact you if that integration becomes available? I will record this requirement for our product team.

Buyer: Yes, that would help.

Human review prompts: Is this a better call despite no purchase? Does honesty about poor product fit count as good performance? Which evidence is product feedback rather than a rep coaching issue?

## Call 3 — non-sales control

Person A: Did you enjoy the walk yesterday? The weather was lovely.

Person B: Yes. Let us meet at the park again on Sunday.

Person A: Sounds good. I will bring some coffee.

Human review prompt: The system should not invent a sales objective or pitch feedback for this conversation.
